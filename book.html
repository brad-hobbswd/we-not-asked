ABOUT THE BOOK

The Book

A biblical examination of authority, responsibility, and how we treat people who are seeking Jesus.

BRADLEY HOBBS

The invitation was never ours to control.

Christ entrusted His church with the gospel, Scripture, discipleship, and faithful service. Yet responsibility can become confused with authority when assumptions, preferences, or judgments begin to stand where Christ never placed them.

We Were Not Asked invites readers to return to the words and character of Jesus, examine how people are treated, and consider whether our conduct reflects the Savior we proclaim.

This is not a call to abandon biblical conviction. It is a call to examine how conviction is expressed, how authority is exercised, and whether our conduct faithfully reflects Christ.
