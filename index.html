A Call to Self-Examination

The heart behind the message

The invitation
was never ours to control.

Christ entrusted His church with the gospel, the teaching of Scripture, discipleship, and the responsibility to serve faithfully. Yet responsibility can become confused with authority when our assumptions, preferences, or judgments begin to stand where Christ never placed them.

We Were Not Asked invites readers to return to the words and character of Jesus, examine the ways we treat people, and consider whether our conduct reflects the Savior we proclaim.

The book

Truth. Responsibility.
The heart of Jesus.

Through biblical examination and honest reflection, this book explores spiritual authority, rejection, church hurt, accountability, compassion, repentance, and the consequences of placing unnecessary barriers before people seeking God.

This is not a call to abandon biblical conviction. This is a call to examine how conviction is expressed, how authority is exercised, how people are treated, and whether our conduct faithfully reflects Christ.

About the author

Bradley Hobbs

Bradley Hobbs writes from a desire to bring Scripture into honest conversation with the lived experiences of people within the church. His work invites readers toward biblical reflection, humility, compassion, accountability, and a deeper understanding of the character of Jesus.

We Were Not Asked is an invitation to examine ourselves with the same sincerity we bring to examining others.
